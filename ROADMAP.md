# Roadmap

## v0.1 — Personal skills registry

Implemented: searchable ownership-aware catalog; three bounded EXPERIMENTAL procedures; preserved deprecated change-review; canonical external links; selective non-overwriting installer; deterministic validator and focused tests. Actual verification and publication state are in [knowledge/verification.md](knowledge/verification.md).

## Next gates

1. Confirm live Codex discovery on Linux, then WSL, without duplicate names. Record runtime/version and actual invocation evidence.
2. Pilot repo-audit, evidence-contract and change-verifier on authorized disposable cases modeled on ShopClose and Black Lantern. Freeze task, artifacts, rubric and runtime. Retain failures and negative results.
3. Run controlled skill-versus-baseline comparisons before claiming an improvement or promoting to EVALUATED. Promote to STABLE only after explicit acceptance within documented limits.
4. Verify exact manifests and ownership in external repositories before adding installation support. Keep business-strategy methodology in its canonical repository.
5. Add domain procedures only after repeated evidence justifies them: local inference, SSH/Tailscale environments, HVAC integrations, or approval-first social work. These are proposed domains, not implemented capabilities.

Notion remains an optional human research source; no sync, ingestion service or alternate policy source is planned for v0.1.
